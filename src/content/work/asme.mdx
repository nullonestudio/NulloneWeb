## The story

Student chapters run on volunteer energy — and too much of it was going into admin. Registrations lived in forms, payments in screenshots, certificates in a design tool and check-ins on paper.

### What we built

- **Membership & events platform.** One place for members, events, payments and certificates.
- **Automation.** Confirmation emails, reminders and certificates generate themselves.
- **QR check-in.** Volunteers scan tickets on their phones; dashboards update live.
- **AR exhibit guide.** Visitors point their phones at projects to see animated explanations and the team behind them.

### The result

Organisers cut manual admin by around 90% and can launch a new event in a day.
