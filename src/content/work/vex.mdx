## The story

VEX started as a promising prototype: a minimalist arena game where every match is short, sharp and skill-based. The founders knew the core loop was fun. They also knew it wouldn't survive contact with real players and real phones.

### What we did

- **Deterministic netcode.** Rebuilt the simulation so matches stay in sync across devices with very different performance.
- **A performance budget.** Strict draw-call and memory limits, GPU instancing and a monochrome art direction that looks striking and renders cheaply.
- **Meta-game and progression.** Ranked play, unlockable cosmetics and daily challenges that give players a reason to return.
- **Live ops tooling.** Remote config and analytics so the team can balance the game without shipping a new build.

### The result

VEX launched on iOS and Android holding 60fps on mid-range Android phones, with a median match latency under 80ms.
