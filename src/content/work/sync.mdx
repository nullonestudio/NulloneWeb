## The story

Shift-based teams live on their schedules — and they often check them in basements, warehouses and building sites with no signal. Sync needed to be trustworthy offline first, and pretty second.

### Architecture

- **Shared domain layer.** One TypeScript package defines schedules, shifts and rules, used by both the React Native app and the Next.js admin panel.
- **Conflict-free sync.** Edits made offline are merged deterministically when devices reconnect — no lost changes, no double bookings.
- **Weekly builds.** TestFlight and Play internal builds every Friday, so the client could test in the field from week two.

### The result

Sync went from idea to iOS, Android and web in fourteen weeks, with a 99.9% sync success rate and a 70% drop in scheduling conflicts for pilot teams.
