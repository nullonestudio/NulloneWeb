## The story

The client's operations team was the engine of the business — and their tools were holding them back. Every loan application passed through a dashboard built years earlier, patched by several different vendors and never designed as a whole.

We started by sitting with analysts for a week. The most important finding wasn't technical: analysts didn't need more data, they needed **the right data on one screen**, in the order they actually make decisions.

### What we changed

- **One review screen.** Applicant, documents, risk signals and history together — no more tab-hopping.
- **Keyboard-first.** Approve, flag and assign without touching the mouse.
- **Shareable state.** Every filter and view lives in the URL, so a link is enough to hand over a case.
- **A monochrome design system.** Colour is reserved for meaning — risk, status, alerts — so it actually stands out.

### Under the hood

The new dashboard runs on Next.js with server components for fast first loads, a typed API layer shared with the backend, and streaming updates so queues stay current without refreshing.
